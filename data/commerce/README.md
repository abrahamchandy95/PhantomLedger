# Commerce reference-data design

## Decision

PhantomLedger must **not** ingest or maintain a nationwide `merchants.csv`
containing real merchant identities. There is no such input file today:
`merchants.csv` is a legacy table stem used by the standard exporter, while
the world catalogue is generated by `synth::merchants::makeCatalog`.

The target is a hybrid model:

1. pin public aggregate facts about population and establishments;
2. deterministically synthesize merchant organizations, physical outlets,
   and remote acceptance endpoints from those aggregates; and
3. keep the generated merchant identities synthetic and seed-replayable.

This retains coverage of the long tail without licensing, privacy,
survivorship, or update problems from a literal list of real businesses.

The IBM benchmark supports this hierarchy rather than a short merchant list.
Its [released TabFormer corpus](https://github.com/IBM/TabFormer) references
100,343 merchant identifiers, while the underlying [virtual-world generator
paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/1910.03033) describes more than 300 multinational
organizations and more than 16 million merchant locations. PhantomLedger
should reproduce the organization/outlet/endpoint *structure and statistical
supply*, not those identities.

## Current gap

The current generator chooses each merchant category uniformly, gives it a
lognormal popularity weight, and places physical records in one of 71 US city
rows using population alone. `localOutlet`, `regionalOutlet`, and
`nationalService` are distinct enum values, but the card-present geographic
pool currently applies the same distance kernel to all records with a valid
location. The catalogue therefore has causal geography, but not an empirical
local supply surface or a brand/outlet/channel hierarchy.

Those 71 US rows sum to only about 52.9 million residents, so normalizing their
weights represents selected city cores rather than the US population.
`data/geo/us_cities.csv` is a runnable placeholder. It is not a complete
Census place or ZCTA extract, and its `GeoArea.landAreaKm2` field remains zero
because the loader does not currently read a land-area column. Row position is
also the current `GeoAreaId`; inserting a row therefore rekeys every later
area. Expanded data must use stable source-derived keys.

## Pinned public inputs

Normalize only the columns the model consumes and record the source URL,
vintage, retrieval date, SHA-256, transformation version, and license/public-
domain status in a checked-in manifest.

| Model fact | Preferred source | Use |
|---|---|---|
| geographic ids, land area, representative coordinates | [US Census Gazetteer files](https://www.census.gov/geographies/reference-files/time-series/geo/gazetteer-files.html) | canonical ZCTA/place geometry inputs |
| residential population | [ACS 5-year detailed tables, B01003](https://api.census.gov/data/2023/acs/acs5/groups.html) | home-area sampling and density |
| employer establishments by ZIP and NAICS | [County/ZIP Code Business Patterns](https://www.census.gov/programs-surveys/cbp/data/datasets.html) | physical outlet supply by industry and geography |
| postal-business to tract/county allocation | [HUD-USPS ZIP Crosswalk](https://www.huduser.gov/portal/datasets/usps_crosswalk.html) | bridge business ZIP mass without pretending ZIP equals ZCTA |
| firms without paid employees | [Nonemployer Statistics](https://www.census.gov/data/developers/data-sets/nonemp-api.html) | county-level independent/sole-proprietor long tail |
| in-person versus remote card share | [Federal Reserve Payments Study](https://www.federalreserve.gov/paymentsystems/fr-payments-study.htm) | date-varying acceptance-channel demand, not outlet supply |

Census demographic products use ZCTAs, while business products use reported
USPS ZIP Codes. They are not the same geography. The build step must preserve
that distinction: exact five-digit matches may be used with an explicit match
flag; unmatched business ZIPs fall back through a pinned ZIP-to-county bridge
and are allocated among county ZCTAs. Never silently claim that a ZBP ZIP is a
Census ZCTA. See the [Census ZIP/ZCTA guidance](https://www.census.gov/data/what-is-data-census-gov/guidance-for-data-users/frequently-asked-questions/how-can-i-find-data-for-zip-codes-on-data-census-gov.html).

## Normalized build artifacts

CSV remains the preferred checked-in format for these flat, projected tables.
Parquet is justified only if a future panel retains enough annual/NAICS detail
to make CSV size or parse time material.

### `geo_areas.csv`

One canonical area per row:

`area_key,area_type,source_geoid,state_fips,state_code,latitude_e6,longitude_e6,population,population_moe,land_area_m2,urban_class,gazetteer_vintage,acs_vintage`

Use stable keys such as `US-ZCTA-02138`, then canonical-sort by that key before
assigning compact runtime ids. Integer coordinates and integer square metres
preserve source precision; density is derived, not stored.

Keep place/city identity separate:

- `places.csv`:
  `place_geoid,state_fips,place_name,place_type,latitude_e6,longitude_e6,land_area_m2,population,population_moe,vintage`
- `area_place_links.csv`:
  `area_key,place_geoid,overlap_weight,is_primary_display_place,assignment_method,quality_flag`

A ZCTA may overlap multiple places, and multiple ZCTAs may map to one place.
Rural/unincorporated areas need an explicit county-based display label. Never
copy a ZCTA population into a City vertex merely because that City is chosen
as its display label.

### `establishment_cells.csv`

Aggregated supply cells, not merchant identities:

`vintage,business_zip,county_fips,naics,employment_size_class,establishments,employment,annual_payroll,disclosure_code`

Retain disclosure/suppression metadata. A missing or suppressed value is not
zero. Allocate postal ZIP business mass through a pinned HUD-USPS business-
address crosswalk and reconcile the result to county CBP control totals.
Project CBP to the NAICS levels needed by the model before checking the
artifact in. Exact ZIP/ZCTA string matching is a tagged fallback, never an
implicit geographic join.

### `merchant_category_crosswalk.csv`

An audited model mapping:

`naics,model_category,mcc_family,physical_capable,remote_capable,reach_policy`

NAICS describes the establishment's industry; MCC describes the card
acceptance category. They are related but not interchangeable. Every mapping
is a classed CHOICE row in `docs/fraud_model_audit.md` until validated against
a publishable network or issuer crosswalk.

### `payment_channel_anchors.csv`

Evidence points rather than a fabricated annual series:

`year,remote_share_by_count,in_person_chip_share_by_count,population_scope,source_id`

The simulator may interpolate only between documented anchors. Extrapolated
years and pandemic discontinuities must be explicit policy, with tests at
every anchor.

## World model

Keep three concepts separate even if the first implementation stores them in
one compact catalogue:

- **MerchantOrganization** — a synthetic brand/owner with category and size;
- **MerchantOutlet** — a physical acceptance location in a canonical area;
- **MerchantAcceptanceEndpoint** — the thing a transaction targets, carrying
  `inPerson` and/or `remote` capability and optionally referencing an outlet.

Large chains may own many outlets; independent organizations usually own one.
A remote endpoint has no invented physical transaction location. An
omnichannel organization can have both outlet endpoints and a remote endpoint.
The existing TigerGraph `Merchant` id should identify the acceptance endpoint
until the schema explicitly adds Brand/Outlet vertices.

Generate outlet quotas from establishment cells with deterministic rounding
and named RNG lanes. Generate popularity separately from supply: establishment
count controls how many outlets exist, while payment-volume/category evidence
controls transaction weights. Do not make either quantity uniform by category.

For the 1991--2019 target, organizations and outlets also need effective-date
intervals. Use annual aggregate vintages plus documented entry/survival policy;
do not let a 2024 establishment surface exist unchanged in 1991. NAICS revision
bridges and any backcast before the selected source panel are explicit CHOICE
rows in the model authority. A first runnable version may use coarse yearly
birth/death hazards, but its assumptions and coverage gaps remain technical
debt until checked against historical Business Dynamics Statistics/CBP totals.
The official CBP downloads cover county data from 1986, but ZIP-level files
begin in 1994; the 1991--1993 endpoint surface therefore needs an explicit
county-to-area allocation/backcast rather than pretending ZIP observations
exist for those years.

## Scalable selection

The present `occupiedAreas x physicalMerchants` CDF is unsuitable for tens of
thousands of ZCTAs. Replace it before expanding the catalogue:

1. index physical outlets by area and reach policy;
2. lazily build pools only for occupied home areas;
3. use a bounded local-neighbour candidate set (plus an explicit regional or
   national tail) and then sample an outlet within the selected area; and
4. maintain separate remote, national-service, regional, and local pools.

Favorites must reference endpoints and remain mode-aware. Monthly popularity
or favorite evolution must rebuild/version the affected sampling weights; the
current production favorite-evolution pass is not implemented.

## Delivery order and acceptance

1. **Provenance round, byte-neutral:** manifest, normalized schemas, loader
   validation, ZIP/ZCTA match reporting, no model reads.
2. **World round, model-moving:** organization/outlet/endpoint synthesis from
   aggregate cells; category and geographic coverage gates; named re-pin.
3. **Transaction-mode round, model-moving:** persist in-person/remote mode and
   make selection plus `use_chip` consequences of that state.
4. **Fraud round, model-moving:** compromised instrument, fraud modality,
   merchant endpoint, device/IP, and geography become one coherent event.

Acceptance gates must cover deterministic replay, every occupied area having a
documented fallback, supply by category/geography matching the normalized
cells within scaling tolerance, no online endpoint with invented outlet
coordinates, no in-person transaction at a remote-only endpoint, and bounded
memory at the 20k/29-year target.
